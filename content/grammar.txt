## file present simple & progresive
TITLE: Present Simple & Present Progressive
HE: הווה פשוט והווה מתמשך
TIP: הווה פשוט (Present Simple) – לעובדות, הרגלים ושגרה. מילים שמרמזות: always, usually, every day.
TIP: הווה מתמשך (Present Progressive) – למה שקורה עכשיו או למצב זמני. מילים שמרמזות: now, at the moment, this week.
TIP: עם he / she / it מוסיפים s לפועל: he works, she eats. בשלילה: doesn't + פועל בלי s.
TIP: פעלי מצב (know, like, believe, own) כמעט אף פעם לא באים עם ing.

Q: I ___ to the gym three times a week.
A: go | am going | goes
WHY: שגרה (three times a week) ← הווה פשוט. עם I לא מוסיפים s.

Q: Sorry, I can't talk now — I ___ a client.
A: am meeting | meet | meets
WHY: קורה עכשיו (now) ← הווה מתמשך: am + פועל עם ing.

Q: She ___ in the pension department.
A: works | is work | work
WHY: עובדה קבועה ← הווה פשוט, ועם she מוסיפים s.

Q: We ___ the insurance policies this week.
A: are reviewing | reviews | is reviewing
WHY: משהו זמני (this week) ← הווה מתמשך. עם we משתמשים ב-are.

Q: My son ___ to school every morning.
A: goes | is going | go
WHY: הרגל (every morning) ← הווה פשוט, ועם he/she/it מוסיפים s (go ← goes).

Q: I ___ the answer.
A: know | am knowing | knows
WHY: know הוא פועל מצב – לא אומרים am knowing.

Q: He ___ like coffee.
A: doesn't | don't | isn't
WHY: שלילה עם he/she/it ← doesn't, ואחריו פועל בלי s.

Q: ___ you work on Fridays?
A: Do | Are | Does
WHY: שאלה על שגרה ← Do + you + פועל.

Q: ___ you watching the game right now?
A: Are | Do | Does
WHY: right now ← הווה מתמשך, השאלה מתחילה ב-Are.

Q: Interest rates ___ these days.
A: are rising | rise | rises
WHY: מגמה ושינוי (these days) ← הווה מתמשך.

A: I own two apartments. | I am owning two apartments.
WHY: own הוא פועל מצב – אומרים I own, בלי ing.

A: He usually drinks tea in the morning. | He usually drink tea in the morning. | He is usually drink tea in the morning.
WHY: הרגל (usually) ← הווה פשוט, ועם he מוסיפים s: drinks.

A: I manage the sales team. | I am managing the sales team every year.
WHY: תפקיד קבוע ← הווה פשוט. הווה מתמשך לא מתאים לדבר שקורה כל שנה.

## file 1
TITLE: didn't + base verb
HE: עבר בשלילה: didn't + פועל בצורת בסיס
TIP: בשלילה בעבר משתמשים ב-didn't, והוא כבר "מסמן" שזה עבר.
TIP: לכן אחרי didn't הפועל חוזר לצורת הבסיס: didn't go ולא didn't went, didn't know ולא didn't knew.
TIP: אותו כלל בשאלה: Did you call? ולא Did you called?
TIP: במשפט חיובי בעבר משתמשים בצורת העבר הרגילה: I went, I knew, I called.

Q: I didn't ___ the email yesterday.
A: send | sent | sends
WHY: אחרי didn't – צורת בסיס: send.

Q: She didn't ___ to the meeting.
A: come | came | comes
WHY: אחרי didn't – צורת בסיס: come (לא came).

Q: We didn't ___ the contract.
A: sign | signed | signs
WHY: אחרי didn't לא מוסיפים ed: didn't sign.

Q: Did he ___ the new policy?
A: buy | bought | buys
WHY: בשאלה עם Did – צורת בסיס: buy (לא bought).

Q: My wife didn't ___ the news.
A: hear | heard | hears
WHY: אחרי didn't – צורת בסיס: hear.

Q: Yesterday I ___ to the gym.
A: went | go | didn't went
WHY: משפט חיובי בעבר ← went. רק אחרי didn't חוזרים ל-go.

Q: ___ you call the client yesterday?
A: Did | Do | Were
WHY: שאלה בעבר (yesterday) ← Did + you + צורת בסיס.

A: They didn't pay the premium. | They didn't paid the premium.
WHY: אחרי didn't – צורת בסיס: pay, ולא paid.

A: I didn't go to the gym on Monday. | I didn't went to the gym on Monday.
WHY: אחרי didn't – go, ולא went.

A: He didn't understand the question. | He didn't understood the question.
WHY: אחרי didn't – understand, ולא understood.

## file 2
TITLE: all vs whole
HE: all או whole
TIP: all – הרבה דברים או אנשים יחד: all the houses, all my clients.
TIP: whole – דבר אחד שלם, מההתחלה עד הסוף: the whole day, the whole building.
TIP: שימו לב לסדר: all + the + ...  לעומת  the + whole + ...

Q: ___ my clients renewed their policies.
A: All | The whole | Whole
WHY: הרבה לקוחות (רבים) ← All.

Q: I read the ___ report last night.
A: whole | all | all of
WHY: דוח אחד, מההתחלה עד הסוף ← the whole report.

Q: We spent the ___ weekend at my parents' house.
A: whole | all | all the
WHY: סוף שבוע אחד שלם ← the whole weekend.

Q: ___ the apartments in the building are sold.
A: All | Whole | The whole
WHY: הרבה דירות (רבים) ← All the apartments.

Q: He ate the ___ pizza by himself.
A: whole | all | all of
WHY: פיצה אחת שלמה ← the whole pizza.

Q: ___ the kids are at school today.
A: All | Whole | The whole
WHY: כמה ילדים יחד ← All the kids.

Q: The ___ family came to my birthday.
A: whole | all | all of
WHY: משפחה אחת שלמה ← the whole family.

A: All my files are on the computer. | Whole my files are on the computer.
WHY: הרבה קבצים ← All my files.

## file 3
TITLE: very vs really
HE: very או really
TIP: very = מאוד. בא רק לפני תואר או תואר הפועל: very tired, very quickly.
TIP: really = ממש / באמת. אפשר לשים אותו גם לפני פועל: I really like it.
TIP: לפני פועל – רק really: I really enjoy it ✓  /  I very enjoy it ✗.

Q: I ___ like my new job.
A: really | very | very much
WHY: לפני פועל (like) ← רק really.

Q: I ___ want to improve my English.
A: really | very | much
WHY: לפני פועל (want) ← really.

Q: He ___ understands the pension market.
A: really | very | much
WHY: לפני פועל (understands) ← really.

Q: Thank you ___ much!
A: very | really | so very
WHY: Thank you very much – ביטוי קבוע.

A: She really enjoys the class. | She very enjoys the class.
WHY: very לא בא לפני פועל ← really enjoys.

A: I really need a vacation. | I very need a vacation.
WHY: need הוא פועל ← really need.

A: We really appreciate your help. | We very appreciate your help.
WHY: appreciate הוא פועל ← really appreciate.

## file 4
TITLE: No "to" after modal verbs
HE: בלי to אחרי can / should / must ודומיהם
TIP: הפעלים can, could, should, must, will, would, may, might נקראים פעלים מודאליים.
TIP: אחריהם בא פועל בצורת בסיס – בלי to: I can help ✓  /  I can to help ✗.
TIP: גם עם he / she לא מוסיפים s: She can swim ✓  /  She cans swim ✗.

Q: I can ___ you with the claim.
A: help | to help | helping
WHY: אחרי can – פועל בצורת בסיס, בלי to.

Q: You should ___ the client today.
A: call | to call | calls
WHY: אחרי should – בלי to: should call.

Q: You must ___ the premium on time.
A: pay | to pay | paying
WHY: אחרי must – צורת בסיס: must pay.

Q: We could ___ a better price.
A: negotiate | to negotiate | negotiated
WHY: אחרי could – צורת בסיס, בלי to.

Q: He might ___ a new apartment.
A: buy | to buy | buys
WHY: אחרי might – צורת בסיס: might buy.

Q: She ___ speak three languages.
A: can | cans | can to
WHY: can לא משתנה עם she, ואחריו לא בא to.

Q: I would ___ the offer.
A: accept | to accept | accepting
WHY: אחרי would – צורת בסיס: would accept.

A: We may need more time. | We may to need more time.
WHY: אחרי may – בלי to.

A: I want to go to the gym. | I want go to the gym.
WHY: want הוא לא פועל מודאלי – אחריו כן צריך to: want to go.

## file 5
TITLE: Adjectives vs Adverbs
HE: שם תואר (Adjective) או תואר הפועל (Adverb)
TIP: Adjective מתאר שם עצם (אדם, דבר, מקום): a happy child, a big house.
TIP: Adverb מתאר פועל (איך עושים משהו), ולרוב נגמר ב-ly: she sings beautifully, he drives carefully.
TIP: יוצאי דופן: good ← well, ו-fast / hard / early / late נשארים בלי ly.
TIP: יש מילים שנגמרות ב-ly והן דווקא שמות תואר: friendly, lovely, costly, daily.

Q: She is a ___ driver.
A: careful | carefully | carefuly
WHY: מתאר את driver (שם עצם) ← Adjective: careful.

Q: She drives ___.
A: carefully | careful | carefull
WHY: מתאר איך היא נוהגת (פועל) ← Adverb: carefully.

Q: He speaks English very ___.
A: well | good | goodly
WHY: מתאר את הפועל speaks ← well (ה-Adverb של good).

Q: My advisor gave me a ___ answer.
A: quick | quickly | quicker
WHY: מתאר את answer (שם עצם) ← quick.

Q: I finished the report ___.
A: quickly | quick | quickness
WHY: מתאר איך סיימתי (פועל) ← quickly.

Q: He works very ___.
A: hard | hardly | hardy
WHY: hard הוא גם Adverb, בלי ly. (hardly = כמעט לא – משמעות אחרת!)

Q: I came home ___ yesterday.
A: late | lately | latly
WHY: late בלי ly הוא ה-Adverb. (lately = לאחרונה – משמעות אחרת.)

Q: The new manager is very ___.
A: friendly | friendlily | friend
WHY: friendly נגמר ב-ly אבל הוא Adjective – מתאר את המנהל.

Q: She is a ___ singer.
A: good | well | goodly
WHY: מתאר את singer (שם עצם) ← good.

A: He runs fast. | He runs fastly.
WHY: fast לא מקבל ly – he runs fast.

## file 6
TITLE: do / does and have / has
HE: do / does ו-have / has
TIP: בשאלות ובשלילה בהווה פשוט משתמשים ב-do, ועם he / she / it ב-does.
TIP: אחרי does הפועל בלי s: Does she play? ✓  /  Does she plays? ✗.
TIP: have = יש לי (I / you / we / they). עם he / she / it אומרים has.
TIP: אבל אחרי does / doesn't חוזרים ל-have: Does he have a car? ✓.

Q: ___ you like coffee?
A: Do | Does | Are
WHY: you ← Do.

Q: ___ your wife work in finance?
A: Does | Do | Is
WHY: your wife = she ← Does.

Q: Does she ___ tennis?
A: play | plays | playing
WHY: אחרי does – הפועל בלי s.

Q: He ___ eat meat.
A: doesn't | don't | isn't
WHY: שלילה עם he ← doesn't.

Q: We ___ a car and a motorcycle.
A: have | has | haves
WHY: we ← have.

Q: My son ___ two cats.
A: has | have | haves
WHY: my son = he ← has.

Q: Does your house ___ a balcony?
A: have | has | having
WHY: אחרי does חוזרים ל-have.

Q: I usually ___ lunch at noon.
A: have | has | do
WHY: have lunch – ביטוי קבוע. עם I ← have.

Q: She ___ yoga every morning.
A: does | do | makes
WHY: do yoga = לעשות יוגה. עם she ← does.

A: Does he have a pension plan? | Does he has a pension plan?
WHY: אחרי does – have, ולא has.

## file Giora Bar (7)
TITLE: will + verb (no "to")
HE: אחרי will – פועל בלי to
TIP: עתיד עם will: Subject + will + verb: I will go.
TIP: אחרי will לא שמים to: I will call you ✓  /  I will to call you ✗.
TIP: עם פעלים אחרים כמו want / need כן צריך to: I want to go, I need to work.

Q: I will ___ you tomorrow.
A: call | to call | calling
WHY: אחרי will – פועל בלי to.

Q: They will ___ next week.
A: arrive | to arrive | arrives
WHY: will + צורת בסיס: will arrive.

Q: She will ___ the contract on Monday.
A: sign | to sign | signs
WHY: אחרי will – בלי to ובלי s.

Q: I need ___ to the bank.
A: to go | go | going
WHY: need הוא לא will – אחריו כן צריך to.

Q: We ___ study English together.
A: will | will to | wills
WHY: will + verb, בלי to.

A: He will help us. | He will to help us.
WHY: אחרי will – בלי to.

A: I want to travel this summer. | I want travel this summer.
WHY: want – כן צריך to: want to travel.

A: My wife will cook dinner tonight. | My wife will to cook dinner tonight.
WHY: will + verb, בלי to.

## file Giora Bar (8)
TITLE: Past Simple & Past Progressive
HE: עבר פשוט ועבר מתמשך
TIP: עבר פשוט (Past Simple) – משהו שקרה בעבר או הרגל מהעבר: I worked yesterday.
TIP: עבר מתמשך (Past Progressive) – was / were + פועל עם ing: I was working.
TIP: אסור לשים was וגם פועל בעבר יחד: I was wanted ✗  ←  I wanted ✓.
TIP: פעולה ארוכה שנקטעה: was + ing ואחריו when + עבר פשוט: I was eating when my mom called.

Q: I ___ yesterday.
A: worked | was worked | was work
WHY: עבר פשוט – פועל אחד בעבר, בלי was.

Q: I ___ to go to the beach.
A: wanted | was wanted | was want
WHY: אסור was + פועל בעבר יחד ← I wanted.

Q: I ___ breakfast when my mom phoned me.
A: was eating | ate | was eat
WHY: פעולה ארוכה שנקטעה ← was + ing.

Q: I was training when my phone ___.
A: rang | was ring | ringing
WHY: הפעולה הקצרה שקטעה ← עבר פשוט.

Q: They ___ at home last night.
A: were | was | are
WHY: they בעבר ← were.

Q: She ___ a student ten years ago.
A: was | were | is
WHY: she בעבר ← was.

Q: What ___ you doing at 7 p.m. yesterday?
A: were | was | did
WHY: you + עבר מתמשך ← were ... doing.

Q: Last weekend I ___ my friends.
A: met | was meet | meeted
WHY: עבר פשוט של meet ← met.

A: I was a trainer. | I was trained a trainer.
WHY: was כאן הוא הפועל היחיד – אין פועל נוסף.

A: We were watching TV when the lights went out. | We watched TV when the lights were going out.
WHY: הפעולה הארוכה ← were watching, הפעולה שקטעה ← went out.
